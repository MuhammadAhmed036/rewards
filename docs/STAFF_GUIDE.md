# Staff guide: the till

For anyone who serves customers. Takes five minutes to read. Print it and keep it by the till.

## Signing in
1. Open your shop's page (the link or QR code your owner gave you) and tap **I work here**.
2. Enter the email and password your owner set up for you.
3. If you forget your password, **ask your owner**: they can set a new one for you in the Staff tab. (The "Forgot password" link is for customers.)
4. Tap **Sign out** when you finish your shift. You are signed out automatically after a few quiet hours.

## Giving points for a purchase
1. Ask the customer to open their card, or to say their customer ID (the letters and numbers under their QR code).
2. Tap **Scan**, point the camera at their QR code. No camera or poor light? Type the customer ID instead.
3. Check the name shown (first name and last initial) is the right person.
4. Type the **amount they spent**. Add the **tip** if there was one (tips don't earn points). If part of the bill doesn't earn points (a gift card, a discounted item), type that in **Part that doesn't earn points**.
5. Read the **They'll get** box. This is exactly what will be added.
6. Tap **Confirm**. Wait for the green success message. **Don't tell the customer it's done until you see it.**

If you tap twice, or the internet drops and you try again, the customer is **not** paid twice. It is safe to retry.

### Messages you might see
| Message | What to do |
|---|---|
| "Earned points a moment ago" | They already earned a few minutes ago. A manager can override with a reason. |
| "Points can't be earned at this location" / "only between 09:00 and 17:00" | Your shop's rules don't allow points here or now. Tell the owner if that looks wrong. |
| "This QR code was already scanned" / "has expired" | Ask the customer to open their card again so it refreshes, or type their customer ID. |
| "You're offline" | Nothing was changed. Wait for the connection, then try again. |
| "Self-award" | You can't give points to your own card. |

## Giving a reward
1. The customer opens their card and taps a reward they can afford. A **one-time code** appears, valid for 10 minutes.
2. Type the code into **Validate reward code**. Check the reward shown.
3. Hand over the reward, then tap **Confirm**. A code works once only.

Never confirm a code before you hand over the reward, and never read a code from a screenshot or photo.

## Something went wrong?
- A customer says points are missing: ask your owner or manager. They can see the history and correct it.
- Wrong person scanned: your manager can reverse it (you can't undo it yourself).
- A customer you don't recognise, many scans in a row, or anyone asking you to "add points for free": say no, and tell your owner.

## Rules of thumb
- Never share your password. Never sign in as someone else.
- Only give points for a real purchase you can see.
- Don't type customers' details anywhere else.
