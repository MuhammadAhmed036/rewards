# Owner guide

Everything you need to run your loyalty programme. Sign in on your shop's page with **I own this shop**.

## First day: set up in this order
1. **Settings → Business**: name, logo, colours, time zone, currency. Your time zone matters: "today", opening hours and daily limits all use it.
2. **Earning rules**: choose how customers earn.
   - *For what they spend* (cafés, salons): "1 point for every £1". Pick rounding, a minimum spend, and whether tips count.
   - *For each visit* (barbers, car washes): "1 point per visit".
   - Set the limits: most points for one purchase, per customer per day, and the wait between earns.
   - Click **Save new rules**. Points already given keep the rule they were earned under.
3. **Rewards**: add what customers can get (cost in points, how many are available, one per customer if you like).
4. **Locations** and **Staff**: add each place you trade, then add your team. Give each person only the role they need.
5. **Test it**: join as a customer with your own other email, give yourself points at the till with a staff account, claim a reward.
6. **Publish** the programme, print your join QR code and put it at the counter.

## Roles
| Role | Can do |
|---|---|
| Owner | Everything for your shop |
| Manager | Run the shop day to day: customers, rewards, corrections |
| Staff | Give points and rewards at the till only |
| Marketing | Send offers; can't change points |

If a staff member forgets their password, open **Staff → Reset password** and set a new one. You will be asked to confirm your own password first: this is for your protection.

## Everyday tasks
- **A customer says points are missing**: Customers → find them → *Adjust points* (add or remove, with a reason). Changes are recorded and can't be hidden.
- **A mistake at the till**: Ledger → find the entry → *Reverse*.
- **A customer is abusing the card**: open their card → *Suspend*, or *Block this email* so they can't rejoin.
- **Lost phone / shared QR**: open their card → *Cancel QR code*. They get a new one.
- **Private notes**: on a customer's card (only you and managers see them; deleted after two years).
- **Import your existing customers**: Customers → *Import customers*. Download the template, fill it in, check the file first, then import. Only mark "yes" to marketing if those customers really agreed.
- **Download reports**: Reports. You'll be asked for your password first.

## Offers (email campaigns)
Offers → choose who gets it (everyone, people who haven't been back, regulars, by level, by location, by how much they earned lately), write the message, pick *Send now* or a time, and optionally cap how many people it reaches. Only customers who said yes to offers are emailed, and every email has a one-click "stop" link. You see how many were delivered, opened their card from it, bounced or stopped offers.

Automatic "we miss you" emails: in Earning rules → Extras, set how many days of quiet before one is sent.

## Growing the programme
- **Bonus offers**: double points on Fridays, a limited-time boost.
- **Levels** (Silver, Gold): customers move up by what they earn over a period, and earn faster at higher levels.
- **Invite a friend**: both get points after the friend's first purchase.
- **Live QR codes** (Settings): each customer's QR changes every 30 seconds so a screenshot is useless. Customers must open their card on their phone.

## Keeping your account safe
- Turn on **Sign-in code** (Settings): after your password, we email you a code. Even if someone steals your password they can't get in.
- Use a long, unique password. Don't share it. Each team member gets their own login.
- Check **Ledger → Worth a look** now and then: it lists awards that were unusually large or fast.
- Settings → *Help from our support team*: our team can only look at your account for the time you choose, and only to read.

## Plan and billing
Settings → *Plan and billing* shows your plan, what you've used and your invoices. If a payment is overdue for more than two weeks, adding new team members, locations, offers and imports pause. **Your customers are never affected** and nothing is ever deleted.

## Getting help
Allow our support team in (Settings → Help from our support team), then contact us. We can see what you see, read-only.
